07 · Before you begin

Clear expectations build trust.

A small FAQ explains how the concept is intended to work without overpromising.

Is Evolmeo therapy or crisis support?

No. The product reference describes Evolmeo as an AI coach, not therapy or crisis support.

Can I choose how I coach?

The concept supports text, voice and avatar session modes, with text presented as the clearest default.

What happens to saved coaching context?

The dashboard exposes saved context and privacy controls so people can review what is carried forward and clear memory.

Can I return to earlier sessions?

Yes. Coaching History is designed as a searchable archive of sessions, takeaways and next steps.

Can pricing change?

Yes. The pricing section is a visual concept and should be connected to approved production plan data before launch.
